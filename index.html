01 / About NCC

Character is built
one challenge at a time.

The National Cadet Corps (NCC) is India's voluntary youth organisation associated with the Armed Forces and functioning under the Ministry of Defence. It helps young people develop discipline, leadership, teamwork, confidence and a spirit of service through training, camps and community activities.

✦ FULL FORM

National Cadet Corps

A youth development organisation promoting unity and discipline.

▦ ESTABLISHED

15 July 1948

Established under the National Cadet Corps Act, 1948.

⌖ HEADQUARTERS

New Delhi

NCC Directorate General headquarters, India.

★ OFFICIAL MOTTO

Unity and Discipline

Ekta aur Anushasan.

THE NCC FRAMEWORK

Vision. Mission. Values.

01

Vision

Empower young people with character, confidence, discipline, leadership and a commitment to nation-building.

02

Mission

Develop organised, trained and motivated youth who can provide leadership and contribute positively to society.

03

Values

Unity, discipline, comradeship, secular outlook, respect, teamwork, responsibility and selfless service.

A LEGACY OF SERVICE

History of NCC

From its establishment in 1948, NCC has continued to support youth development and national integration.

1948

NCC is established

The National Cadet Corps came into existence on 15 July 1948 under the NCC Act, 1948.

1949

Girls join the organisation

The Girls Division was introduced, expanding participation in NCC training.

1980

Motto adopted

“Unity and Discipline” became the official NCC motto.

TODAY

Training for tomorrow

NCC provides opportunities for leadership, adventure, national integration and community service.

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

NCC Divisions

THREE SERVICES · ONE PURPOSE

The Three Wings

01 / ARMY

Army Wing

Ground training, field skills and leadership.

  • Drill and parade
  • Field craft and battle craft
  • Map reading and navigation
  • Physical fitness and obstacle training
  • Teamwork and leadership
02 / NAVAL

Naval Wing

Maritime knowledge and practical water-based skills.

  • Seamanship
  • Boat pulling and sailing
  • Navigation and naval knowledge
  • Water safety
  • Water sports where available
03 / AIR

Air Wing

Aviation awareness and air-related training.

  • Aviation and aerospace awareness
  • Aero-modelling
  • Gliding or flying-related training where available
  • Air Force orientation
  • Technical learning and teamwork

02 / NCC Service Wings

Three wings.
One NCC spirit.

Explore the Army, Naval and Air Wings of the National Cadet Corps. Each wing offers service-specific training while developing discipline, leadership, teamwork and responsibility.

01

LAND • LEADERSHIP • COURAGE

Army Wing

Focuses on drill, physical fitness, field craft, map reading, teamwork and leadership.

About Army Wing

Army Wing training introduces cadets to land-based activities, discipline, outdoor skills and teamwork. Training is conducted under the guidance of authorised NCC instructors.

Training areas

  • Drill and parade practice
  • Physical training and obstacle activities
  • Map reading and field craft
  • Leadership, teamwork and community service
  • Army attachment and eligible NCC camps
02

SEA • SEAMANSHIP • TEAMWORK

Navy Wing

Introduces cadets to seamanship, navigation, sailing, water safety and maritime awareness.

About Naval Wing

Naval Wing training develops an understanding of maritime activities, safe conduct around water, teamwork and basic nautical skills. Practical activities depend on facilities and unit arrangements.

Training areas

  • Basic seamanship and nautical terminology
  • Navigation fundamentals
  • Sailing and water safety, where available
  • Maritime awareness and teamwork
  • Sea attachment and eligible sailing camps
03

AIR • AVIATION • INNOVATION

Air Wing

Develops aviation awareness through aerospace learning, aeromodelling and eligible flying-related activities.

About Air Wing

Air Wing introduces cadets to aviation, aerospace concepts and air-force-related awareness. Practical flying or gliding activities depend on availability, permissions and eligibility.

Training areas

  • Basic aviation and aerospace knowledge
  • Aeromodelling and aircraft awareness
  • Air navigation fundamentals
  • Leadership, discipline and teamwork
  • Eligible air-force attachment and flying camps

Note: Activities, equipment and camp eligibility vary by NCC unit. Follow instructions issued by your unit and authorised instructors.

07 / GET STARTED

How to join NCC.

Enrolment is normally coordinated through a school or college with an NCC unit. The local unit confirms vacancies and the applicable rules.

1

Check your institution

Ask whether your school or college has an NCC unit and which wing is available.

2

Contact the unit

Speak to the Associate NCC Officer (ANO), Caretaker Officer (CTO) or unit staff.

3

Confirm eligibility

Check age, student status, medical/fitness requirements, vacancies and the current enrolment process.

4

Complete the official process

Submit the required form and documents through your institution/unit; follow the instructions you receive.

5

Attend training regularly

Be punctual, maintain attendance, follow safety directions and participate in approved activities.

✦

Before you apply

  • Student ID / proof of enrolment
  • Parent or guardian consent if required
  • Medical or fitness documents if requested
  • Photographs and identity documents as instructed
  • Commitment to regular attendance

This is a preparation list, not a universal official document checklist. Your unit may ask for different items.

08 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

What is the full form of NCC?

NCC stands for National Cadet Corps.

What is the motto of NCC?

“Unity and Discipline” — Ekta aur Anushasan.

Is NCC compulsory?

NCC enrolment is generally voluntary, subject to the rules and arrangements of the institution and unit.

Can girls join NCC?

Yes. Eligible girls can join through the relevant Junior Wing or Senior Wing arrangements available at their institution.

Does joining NCC mean I must join the Armed Forces?

No. NCC develops youth and provides service exposure; cadets are not automatically obligated to join the Armed Forces after completing NCC.

Can I join any wing I want?

Available wings depend on the NCC unit attached to your institution, vacancies and the current enrolment process.

Does an NCC certificate guarantee a job?

No. Any benefit depends on the current recruitment notification and eligibility rules. Always verify directly with the recruiting authority.

How do I know camp dates and selection criteria?

Ask your ANO/CTO or unit. Dates, selection stages and eligibility are communicated through official instructions.

Where can I find official NCC information?

Start with the national portal and your State/UT NCC Directorate. Links are provided below.
